How a campaign moves through ADbridge

One pipeline, three stages — advertiser, promoter, payout.

01

Advertiser adds a product

List your product and choose how promoters get paid — fixed interest or commission.

02

Promoter pays to run it

A promoter selects your product and pays ADbridge to promote it. ADbridge runs the ads and routes every lead into an ADbridge chat.

03

Sale made, payout tracked

When a lead converts to a sale, ADbridge records it and pays out according to the structure the advertiser chose.

Two ways a campaign can pay out

When an advertiser adds a product, they choose one of two payout structures. Promoters see which structure applies before they pay to run it.

Type 1 — Fixed interest campaigns

Fixed interest, on a schedule

  • Advertiser sets a fixed interest rate on the product — e.g. 70%
  • Promoter pays ADbridge — e.g. ₦1,000 — to run the campaign
  • Once reviewed and approved, the promoter's dashboard adds up both the amount they paid and the interest earned
  • Balances are reviewed on the 25th of each month
  • Approved balances can be withdrawn on the 28th
List a fixed-interest product
Type 2 — Commission campaigns

Paid per sale, not upfront

  • Advertiser sets a fixed commission percentage per sale when adding the product — e.g. 20%
  • Promoter pays ADbridge to run the campaign — e.g. ₦1,000
  • Once the first sale is made, the promoter gets their ₦1,000 back, plus that commission percentage on that sale and every sale after
  • If the campaign generates no sales, the promoter loses the ₦1,000 they paid
