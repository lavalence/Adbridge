03

Two ways a campaign can pay out

When an advertiser funds a campaign, they choose one of two payout structures. Promoters see which structure applies before they pick up the campaign.

Type 1 — Bonus campaigns

Fixed bonus, on a schedule

  • Advertiser funds a campaign — e.g. ₦1,000 — and sets a bonus rate, e.g. 70%
  • Once the campaign is reviewed and approved, the promoter's dashboard shows ₦1,700
  • Balances are reviewed on the 25th of each month
  • Approved balances can be withdrawn on the 28th
Fund a bonus campaign
Type 2 — Revenue-share campaigns

Paid per sale, not upfront

  • Advertiser funds the campaign to get it listed and promoted
  • Promoter earns 50% of every sale the campaign generates
  • If the campaign generates no sales, the promoter earns nothing from it
  • Every payout is tied to a verified sale, tracked in real time
